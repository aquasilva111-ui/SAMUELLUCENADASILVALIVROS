# 8. Sigilo branco sem fundo — versão criada

## Objetivo autoral
“transformar isso numa ilustração vector sem fundo”  
Feedback: “de cor branca”

## Prompt registrado
“Minimalist white vector sigil on a transparent background: a centered, symmetrical double-ring seal with crisp uniform strokes, classical serif letters spaced around the outer ring, and no scenery or additional elements. Inside, a bold cross-like occult glyph features flared cross-potent terminals and a smooth serpentine hook curving down toward the lower right, rendered flat with sharp edges, clean curves, and no shading or texture.”

## Observação
O resultado criado é rasterizado apesar da intenção “vector”. Para arquivo vetorial real, precisa ser redesenhado/exportado em SVG.

## Próximo passo visual
Produzir SVG manual com:
- traço branco puro;
- fundo totalmente transparente;
- círculos regulares;
- letras vetorizadas ou convertidas em paths;
- símbolo central redesenhado geometricamente;
- versão preta correspondente;
- versão simplificada para tamanho pequeno.

# 11. Conceitos para imagens editoriais futuras

## 11.1 O escudo vira músculo
Visual: camada de metal/espelho deixando de cobrir uma figura e entrando como fibra no corpo.

Prompt-base:
“Editorial magical realism illustration of a feminine figure whose reflective shield is dissolving into muscle fibers under the skin, symbolizing a persona becoming an embodied capacity. Dark garden background, ritual atmosphere, elegant rather than violent, conceptual visual metaphor, high-fashion editorial composition.”

## 11.2 Petróleo Coletivo
Visual: massa escura subterrânea subindo por tubos até um palco.

Prompt-base:
“Conceptual editorial illustration of collective emotion as black luminous petroleum beneath a city, rising through symbolic pipes toward a pop stage where it is refined into light, music, flowers and movement. No industrial realism; mythic psychological diagram disguised as fashion editorial.”

## 11.3 A deusa encontra infraestrutura
Visual: relevo antigo de deusa conectado a telas, cabos, flashes, celulares e palco.

Prompt-base:
“Ancient feminine archetype emerging from weathered stone and connecting to contemporary screens, cameras, stage lights, notifications and crowds, showing an old goddess discovering modern mass-media infrastructure. Esoteric but editorial, dark botanical environment, red and magenta accents.”

## 11.4 Não se recebe metade de uma deusa
Visual: figura dividida por luz e sombra, sem moralizar os lados.

Prompt-base:
“Editorial symbolic portrait of a goddess whose luminous and shadow aspects are inseparable in the same body, no good-versus-evil cliché, one continuous figure carrying autonomy, desire, rage, isolation, ambition and tenderness. Ritual fashion photography aesthetic.”

## 11.5 A pele no chão
Visual: pele de serpente translúcida vazia e serpente viva seguindo adiante.

Prompt-base:
“Quiet magical-realist editorial scene: a translucent shed snakeskin resting on dark earth in a garden, while the living serpent moves forward beside a woman walking away. No horror, no dead snake, transformation rather than death, dawn light, contemplative ending.”
