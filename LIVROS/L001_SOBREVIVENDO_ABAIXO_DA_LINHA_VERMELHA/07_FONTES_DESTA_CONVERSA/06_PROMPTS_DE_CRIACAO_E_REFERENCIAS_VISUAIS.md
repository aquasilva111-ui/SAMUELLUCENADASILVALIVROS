## PROMPT LN-01 — The Red Line / Physiological Treasure
**Prompt (EN):** Conceptual editorial illustration for a scientific-dramatic nonfiction book: a human-shaped chamber sheltering a luminous reservoir of living energy, but its own walls are being sold piece by piece to pay for a small roof above it. A perfectly horizontal red line marks the threshold of security. Minimal forms, powerful emotional legibility, no gore, no poverty stereotypes, no words in artwork. Muted charcoal, intense vermilion, restrained orange. Broad negative space, sophisticated book-jacket art.
**Mensagem:** proteger o tesouro fisiológico, não gastá-lo para comprar a proteção.

## PROMPT LN-02 — The Overlapping Lines / Maslow as conceptual map
**Prompt (EN):** Refined infographic, layered horizontal zones labeled physiological and safety (red), belonging (yellow), esteem (green), self-actualization (open warm upper field), with human trajectories and uncertainty bands. Clearly label it an author's conceptual adaptation rather than Abraham Maslow's original graphic. Precision typography, space for documented data, accessible contrast, print-ready vector language.
**Mensagem:** não afirmar uma hierarquia rígida nem apresentar o modelo gráfico como origem literal de Maslow.

## PROMPT LN-03 — Two childhoods, one ambition
**Prompt (EN):** Split-frame conceptual visual: two equally luminous developing minds represented as identical seedlings; one has continuous light, shelter, water and soil, another must use its roots to hold up the shelter and capture scarce rain. Avoid pity and biological determinism. Semi-abstract educational illustration, elegantly scientific, emotionally restrained.
**Mensagem:** condições de aprendizado, meritocracia, heterogeneidade dos pontos de partida.

## PROMPT LN-04 — Scarcity Below / Scarcity Above
**Prompt (EN):** Cinematic dual-scale illustration: one world under a red economic horizon balancing rent, food and bodily exhaustion; above it a luminous orbital economy of stations, launch vehicles and distant worlds. One continuous line connects both spheres; do not suggest space expansion is bad, show unequal readiness to participate. Editorial science fiction restraint, not a corporate advertisement; high-contrast geometry.
**Mensagem:** dois horizontes sociais da mesma humanidade.

## PROMPT LN-05 — The Nervous System Economy
**Prompt (EN):** A human silhouette with an understated nervous-system network functioning as a power grid for city activity; some circuitry glows with sustained demand, while quiet protected areas permit restoration. Avoid fabricated neuroscience labels, no diagnostic imagery. Elegant conceptual scientific illustration, disciplined visual hierarchy, neutral medical dignity.
**Mensagem:** custo corporal e social da ativação prolongada.

## PROMPT LN-06 — Orange Economy / Every life begins on solid ground
**Prompt (EN):** Grand but humane utopian civic panorama: universal dignified homes, open public food gardens, accessible clinics, bright shared spaces and people freely practicing crafts, science, art, infrastructure maintenance and care. Orange dawn runs across the entire city, not just its wealthy districts. Warm aspirational atmosphere without luxury branding, propaganda iconography or forced uniformity. Inclusive people shown as agents and citizens, not beneficiaries.
**Mensagem:** sobrevivência como ponto de partida, não prêmio.

## PROMPT LN-07 — Honor of essential professions
**Prompt (EN):** Monumental yet intimate civic portrait series honoring hospital cleaners, sanitation workers, nurses, water engineers and emergency maintainers as celebrated contributors to public life; a cleaning professional centered with equal human dignity to a specialist physician; welcoming architecture and understated orange illumination. No military costume; civic honor through expression, public recognition and fair compensation, not invented insignia.
**Mensagem:** contribuição essencial, honra civil e vocação.
