## IMG-L026-001 — Homem sob vigilância

**Função:** capa conceitual / abertura / divulgação  
**Arquivo:** IMAGEM_001_HOMEM_SOB_VIGILANCIA.svg  
**Status:** proposta

### Conceito
Um homem no centro de um espaço de julgamento. Marcas e olhos abstratos medem corpo, postura, força e comportamento; dentro da figura rígida, uma forma mais fluida tenta expandir-se.

### Prompt V1
Editorial conceptual illustration for a philosophical book titled “A Masculinidade Determinista”. A solitary adult male figure stands front-facing inside a rigid geometric frame that resembles both a suit, an armor and a measurement device. Around him, abstract surveillance marks, arrows and eye-like shapes evaluate posture, strength, heterosexual performance and emotional control. Inside the rigid silhouette, a softer translucent second human form expands sideways, suggesting tenderness, affection, vulnerability and unrealized possibilities. Minimal composition, strong negative space, high conceptual clarity, sophisticated contemporary book-cover language, no literal rainbow symbolism, no caricature, no macho cliché, no explicit sexuality, sensation of identity under permanent examination.

### Negativo
bodybuilder cliché, red-pill meme, rainbow flag as central symbol, erotic scene, explicit nudity, propaganda poster, smiling stock-photo man, comic-book superhero, medical diagram, gore.

## IMG-L026-002 — Ego / Alter Ego / Superego

**Função:** diagrama conceitual  
**Arquivo:** IMAGEM_002_EGO_ALTEREGO_SUPEREGO.svg  
**Status:** proposta

### Conceito
Triângulo de tensão:
- EGO = identidade reconhecida;
- ALTER EGO = experiência não integrada;
- SUPEREGO = vigilância normativa.

No centro: **EU / HOMEM?**

### Prompt V1
Minimal philosophical diagram for a book about deterministic masculinity. Three vertices form a tense triangle around a central human silhouette. The upper vertex is SUPEREGO, represented as a strict observing structure; the left vertex is EGO, represented as a stable public mask; the right vertex is ALTER EGO, represented as a fluid shadow containing tenderness, fear, desire and vulnerability. Thin directional lines show surveillance, repression, compensation and possible integration. Clean editorial vector language, high readability, lots of negative space, conceptual rather than clinical, black-and-white friendly.

## IMG-L026-004 — A mulher como certificado

**Status:** prompt sem imagem criada

Conceptual editorial illustration criticizing the use of women as proof of masculinity. An adult man stands before a social checkpoint holding an official-looking certificate of “male legitimacy”; the document’s silhouette subtly reveals the outline of a woman. The woman herself appears visually separate and autonomous, emphasizing the violence of reducing her to evidence for his identity. Restrained, intelligent, no sexualization.
