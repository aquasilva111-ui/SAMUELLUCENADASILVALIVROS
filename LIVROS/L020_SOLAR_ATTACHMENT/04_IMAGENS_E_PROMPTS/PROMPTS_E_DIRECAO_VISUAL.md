## 3. Prompts-base propostos — NÃO cânone
### 01 — Era anterior a Júpiter
> Concept art for an original mythic science-fantasy novel titled Solar Attachment. A primordial solar system during an age before Jupiter exists; expansive deep-space void amid ancient planetary orbits; visual storytelling through cosmic scale, silence, and absence. Evocative and philosophical, restrained symbolism, cinematic wide composition, no captions, no labels, no invented technology, no recognizable copyrighted franchise aesthetic.

### 02 — A defesa lunar
> Editorial illustration of an intentionally created Moon guarding Earth against distant incoming comets. Communicate cosmic protection using geometry, orbit and contrast, without showing any definitive mechanical explanation. Sacred-cosmic atmosphere, luminous Earth, pale lunar silhouette, distant comet trails, painterly editorial concept art, no text.

### 03 — O Sol oculto
> Mythic solar deity overcome by shame after an unspecified tragedy; the Sun's light hidden by monumental storm clouds, torrents of rain and thunder surrounding the world below. Emotion expressed through silhouette and posture, not through invented costume, biography or exact facial traits. Cinematic, intimate emotion against a planetary scale, no text.

### 04 — Nascimento de Júpiter
> Poetic cosmogenesis for the novel Solar Attachment: an unprecedented immense storm giving rise to the planet Jupiter, lightning and vapor forming a turbulent giant sphere. Show this as fictional mythology rather than scientific illustration. Powerful sense of emergence, atmospheric layers, monumental scale, no labels, no text.

### 05 — Primeiro encontro com a Carruagem
> A young boy named Apollo approaches the colossal Chariot of the Sun under the gaze of an unnamed previous solar guardian. The chariot design remains evocative and deliberately indeterminate, avoid horses or specific mechanisms unless later approved. Vast luminous scale, modest human figure, emotional tension, philosophical fantasy, no text.

### 06 — Aprender a conduzir com justiça
> Symbolic editorial painting of a young Apollo learning to guide the Solar Chariot justly, with the former solar god as mentor. Contrast immense power with the discipline of restraint and responsibility, no literal diagram of seasons or distribution of light, no captions, sophisticated storybook-cinematic atmosphere.
