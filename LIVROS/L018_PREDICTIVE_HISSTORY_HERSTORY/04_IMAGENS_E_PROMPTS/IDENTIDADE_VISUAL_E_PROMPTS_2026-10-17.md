# Predictive Hisstory and Herstory — Identidade Visual e Prompts

**Atualização:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** exploração visual; não confundir com cânone textual.

# 1. DIREÇÃO VISUAL CENTRAL

A obra trabalha com três movimentos:
- **Herstory** como fluxo emergente, orgânico, subterrâneo, vivo;
- **Hisstory** como geometria, arquitetura, redes, planejamento, instituições;
- **History** como superfície resultante da colisão entre ambos.

A identidade deve evitar o clichê “livro de história = pergaminho”. O visual deve comunicar:
- sistema;
- tempo;
- camadas;
- forças;
- causalidade;
- mapas;
- redes;
- símbolos;
- futuro em formação.

# 2. IMAGEM-CONCEITO — A TRÍADE

**Prompt:**

Editorial conceptual illustration for a serious philosophy-of-history book. Three interacting layers: an organic luminous field rising from below, symbolizing Herstory as emergent collective forces; a precise architectural lattice of lines, plans, institutions and strategic nodes descending from above, symbolizing Hisstory as deliberate historical direction; at the collision zone, a dense textured world-map-like surface forms, symbolizing History as manifested reality. No fantasy characters, no literal male/female figures, no conspiracy imagery. Intellectual, museum-quality, elegant, contemporary, high contrast, restrained palette, complex but readable, horizontal 16:9.

# 3. IMAGEM-CONCEITO — CONVENÇÕES DE REALIDADE

**Prompt:**

Conceptual scientific-editorial illustration of a Reality Convention as a gravitational belief ecosystem. A central umbrella-belief node bends a field around itself; clusters of smaller idea-beliefs orbit it, connected by memory, fear, hope, value, desire and experience. New information enters the field: some is absorbed, some reinterpreted, some deflected. Visual language inspired by network science, orbital mechanics and philosophical diagrams, not literal space. Clean dark background, luminous nodes, premium book illustration, no text inside image.

# 4. CAPÍTULO 1 — A ALQUIMIA DA REALIDADE

**Prompt:**

A conceptual editorial image showing an abstract symbol becoming material infrastructure: an idea begins as a faint line in a human perceptual field, becomes a shared symbol, then a contract, a banknote-like abstraction, a border, a school, a city and a nation-scale structure. The transformation must feel like social alchemy, not occult fantasy. Sophisticated historical-philosophical aesthetic, cinematic but analytical.

# 5. CAPÍTULO 2 — COLAPSO

**Prompt:**

Complex civilization depicted as a multilayer network still standing externally while internal load-bearing connections weaken. Financial graphs rise above a stagnating productive layer; elite nodes multiply near the top; bureaucracy thickens into rigid pathways; trust connections dim; multiple shocks approach simultaneously. The image should communicate slow decline followed by sudden systemic rupture, without apocalyptic clichés.

# 6. CAPÍTULO 3 — GERONTOCRACIA

**Prompt:**

Editorial metaphor of generational political economy: an old, asset-heavy upper platform filled with houses, portfolios, institutions and protected infrastructure rests above a younger generation trying to climb through narrowing access points. The older layer is not villainous or grotesque; the visual tension is structural, showing preservation versus entry, past versus future. Contemporary economics-book illustration, clean geometry, human but non-caricatural.

# 9. CAPA — DIREÇÃO A

**Prompt:**

Book cover for “Predictive Hisstory and Herstory”. A world-history system represented as intersecting organic currents and geometric strategic grids. The organic current rises like roots, memory, myth and collective emotion; the grid resembles maps, institutions, capital networks and planned futures. Their collision creates a new historical surface. Minimal but intellectually dense, prestigious academic-trade nonfiction, bold typography space, no faces, no clichéd clocks, no parchment, no Illuminati symbols.

# 10. CAPA — DIREÇÃO B

**Prompt:**

High-concept nonfiction book cover: history as a three-layer machine. Bottom layer: emergent human currents and symbols. Middle layer: institutional diagrams and strategic networks. Top layer: an unstable map of the future assembling itself. Clean modern editorial design, museum catalogue sophistication, philosophical and geopolitical mood, strong negative space for title.
