# Prompts Visuais — L025

**Status:** propostas visuais iniciais, não aprovadas como identidade final  
**Última atualização:** 2026-10-17

## Direção visual

A linguagem visual deve mostrar que um país aparentemente uno contém várias estruturas de poder sobrepostas: anatomia, camadas, soberania fragmentada, mapa interno, fluxo de influência, captura e distribuição. Evitar estética eleitoral partidária genérica.

## L025-VIS-001 — Capa “Anatomia de uma Democracia”

**Prompt:** Book cover concept, a monumental transparent civic structure resembling a parliament or abstract state apparatus, cut open like an anatomical diagram, its interior divided into distinct interlocking chambers representing democratic power, wealth power, oligarchic networks, hereditary elite power, religious authority, bureaucracy, military coercion and executive concentration; democracy is not shown as destroyed but as one living system competing with other systems inside the same national body; sophisticated editorial political theory aesthetic, conceptual illustration, strong geometry, large negative space for title, restrained detail, no flags, no partisan symbols, no politicians, intellectually serious, visual metaphor first, elegant print design, vertical 2:3 composition.

**Negative:** campaign poster, ballot box cliché, red versus blue, propaganda, politician portraits, conspiracy board, comic-book villains.

## L025-VIS-002 — DNA político

**Prompt:** Editorial infographic showing the political DNA of a country as a clean circular composition divided into shares of democratic, plutocratic, oligarchic, aristocratic, theocratic, autocratic, bureaucratic, technocratic and military power; communicate coexistence rather than mutually exclusive regime labels; scientific visualization feel, readable hierarchy, flat vector geometry, labels modular and replaceable, neutral visual language, print-ready.

## L025-VIS-003 — Mapa Setorial do Poder

**Prompt:** A twelve-sector political power matrix around a central node labeled Popular Sovereignty, sectors for elections, executive, legislature, judiciary, economy, administration, information, parties, territory, civil society, religion and coercive power; each sector displays degree of democratic control and type of non-democratic capture; precise systems diagram, editorial data visualization, clean arrows, modular design.

## L025-VIS-004 — País por fora, regimes por dentro

**Prompt:** Conceptual editorial illustration of a nation represented as a single geometric shell labeled Democracy, partially transparent, revealing nested engines and chambers inside: wealth networks, elite councils, inherited corridors, religious authority, bureaucracy, military command and popular assemblies; formal regime label hiding heterogeneous internal structures; analytical atmosphere, not conspiratorial, strong silhouette, concept over detail.

## L025-VIS-005 — Entre eleições

**Prompt:** Conceptual illustration of two election days shown as distant pillars, with the entire space between them filled by institutions, courts, corporations, media systems, agencies, parties, lobbying networks, civic organizations and security structures; ordinary citizens are connected to some nodes and excluded from others; the visual question is how much power remains accessible to the public between elections.

## L025-VIS-006 — Atlas mundial

**Prompt:** World atlas interface for political sovereignty distribution, each country represented not by one democracy score but by selectable layers for democratic control, plutocratic influence, oligarchic concentration, hereditary elite reproduction, religious authority, executive autocracy, military autonomy and information concentration; neutral analytic cartography, uncertainty visible, date and methodology version visible.

## Regra visual

Qualquer imagem com porcentagens deve indicar país, período, versão da metodologia, margem de incerteza e se os números são reais, demonstrativos ou hipotéticos.
