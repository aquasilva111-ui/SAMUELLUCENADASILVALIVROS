## 3. Prompts editoriais reconstituídos
Os textos seguintes **não são transcrições literais dos parâmetros internos** dos modelos usados: são novos prompts editoriais, com base nas imagens e instruções autorais.

### P01 — Capa amarela independente
Original Portuguese nonfiction book cover. Brilliant solid saturated yellow ground. Bold precisely typeset three-line white title “Lei da / mente / privada”, generous editorial spacing, upper-left circular series mark, black ORIGINAL abstract human head illustration on the right, stippling or geometric shading, no reproduction, tracing or imitation of any existing historical illustration, unmistakably new composition; title and author typeset separately, strong legibility in Amazon thumbnail, vertical 2:3.

### P02 — Retrato cartográfico inventado
Create a new frontal human portrait in monochrome pointillist ink, neutral expression, intricate yet entirely independently invented abstract lines and nodes across cranium, symmetric silhouette and slightly asymmetric interior mapping. Avoid copying known phrenology diagrams or antique illustrations. White background, print-ready silhouette, no letters.

### P03 — Firewall do eu
Book illustration, philosophical symbol of psychological boundaries, black linear human profile against bright yellow, organic abstract circuitry appearing only inside the silhouette, wide negative space to the left, typography added in graphic design software. Not a medical anatomy diagram.

### P04 — Desagregação do ruído
Abstract human profile whose external perimeter partly breaks into microscopic ink particles while the conceptual center stays coherent; yellow-black-white graphic palette; original invented stippling field, restrained but bold, literary cover art, without text.

### P05 — Gravura com jurisdição
Ink illustration of an original neutral face, front view, scalp covered in a newly invented map of arched “jurisdiction” boundaries rather than real brain anatomy, densely detailed stippling shading in jaw and neck, sparse white background, sharp high-resolution print texture, not a copy or trace.

## 4. Considerações de licenças
Uma alternativa gerada por IA ou redesenho visualmente semelhante não garante ausência de conflitos autorais. Validar origem e licença de imagens usadas como referência; produzir soluções substancialmente originais; verificar marcas e o contexto dos direitos conforme plataforma/território. Registrar arquivo, data, prompt editorial, feedback, modelo gerador quando conhecido e escolha final. Todos os estudos seguem como protótipos, nenhuma capa final aprovada.
