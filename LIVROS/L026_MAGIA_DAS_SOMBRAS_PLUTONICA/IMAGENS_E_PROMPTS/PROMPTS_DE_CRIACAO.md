# DIREÇÃO VISUAL GERAL

O centro visual não deve ser “Plutão = mal”. Deve ser:
- poder profundo;
- matéria sob pressão;
- reserva;
- transformação;
- riqueza subterrânea;
- energia concentrada;
- fluxo capturado;
- autonomia sendo convertida em dependência;
- produção coletiva e concentração vertical.

Evitar reduzir o livro a:
- caveiras;
- demônios genéricos;
- fogo infernal;
- vilões de cartola;
- estética conspiratória;
- pilhas de dinheiro como símbolo único;
- “witchcore” genérico;
- signos astrológicos aleatórios;
- imagens que façam parecer que riqueza em si é condenada.

# PR-L026-001 — CAPA CONCEITUAL: A POTÊNCIA CAPTURADA

**Data:** 2026-10-17  
**Função:** capa / key art  
**Status:** prompt de exploração

## Intenção
Fazer o espectador sentir que existe uma força enorme produzida por muitos pontos e progressivamente canalizada para um único centro. A imagem deve sugerir poder, profundidade e extração sem transformar os produtores em vítimas caricaturais nem o centro em “demônio literal”.

## Conceito central
Muitos produzem. Um sistema controla o fluxo. O fluxo se concentra. A concentração torna-se poder.

## Prompt
Editorial philosophical book cover for “Magia das Sombras Plutônica”. A vast field of many luminous human-scale sources, workshops, hands, roots, rivers of ore, data-like streams and abstract productive energy feeding into a monumental deep central structure below the earth. The central structure does not look like a monster; it looks like an elegant machine-temple of concentration, ancient and modern at once, absorbing and reorganizing the flows. Visual theme: power, extraction, subterranean wealth, transformation, autonomy versus domination. Sophisticated magical-philosophical realism, symbolic rather than literal, strong negative space for title, restrained planetary glyph of Pluto only if compositionally justified, no random occult symbols, no conspiracy poster aesthetic, no money rain, no cartoon billionaire. The image should communicate the equation: POTÊNCIA DO OUTRO → CONTROLE → EXTRAÇÃO → CONCENTRAÇÃO. Concept before decoration, sensation before excessive detail.

## Elementos obrigatórios
- múltiplas fontes de potência;
- fluxo visível;
- centro de concentração;
- sensação subterrânea/profunda;
- espaço para tipografia.

## Elementos proibidos
- Plutão tratado como demônio;
- pentagramas aleatórios;
- cifrões gigantes;
- caricatura de classe;
- texto ilegível gerado por IA.

# PR-L026-002 — CAPA ABSTRATA: O POÇO DE CONCENTRAÇÃO

**Função:** capa alternativa  
**Data:** 2026-10-17

## Intenção
Representar concentração sem precisar mostrar pessoas.

## Prompt
Minimal conceptual cover about Plutonian shadow magic as concentration of captured power. Many thin streams of mineral light, metallic dust, roots, water, labor-like geometric pulses and faint symbols of resources move horizontally across a wide dark field, then bend downward into a single vertical gravitational well. The well is beautiful, powerful and dangerous because of its asymmetry, not because it is evil-looking. Deep geological layers, polished editorial illustration, philosophical occultism, modern syncretic magic, controlled composition, high conceptual clarity, large quiet areas, subtle Pluto symbolism, no generic zodiac wheel, no fantasy character, no gothic horror, no lava planet.

# PR-L026-003 — RIQUEZA SUBTERRÂNEA E TRANSFORMAÇÃO

**Função:** ilustração interna / conceito de força plutônica  
**Status:** importante porque não representa apenas sombra.

## Intenção
Mostrar que Plutão possui potência própria e não é inerentemente maligno.

## Prompt
Conceptual editorial illustration of Plutonian power before its shadow deformation: deep underground layers containing minerals, pressure, roots, fossils, dormant seeds, heat and hidden reservoirs being transformed into usable forms. No villain, no extraction machine, no domination. The feeling is depth, regenerative pressure, death becoming material for new life, hidden wealth, transformation and concentrated force. Philosophical magical realism, scientifically inspired geological textures without pretending to be a scientific diagram, restrained symbolic language, sophisticated book illustration.

## Uso editorial
Pode acompanhar a seção “Plutão como força” para evitar que a iconografia do livro iguale Plutão à sombra.

# PR-L026-004 — EXTRAÇÃO: O FLUXO MUDA DE DONO

**Função:** ilustração didática

## Prompt
Horizontal conceptual sequence in one unified editorial image. On the left, distributed productive sources generate distinct streams: work, land, infrastructure, knowledge, creativity, attention and material resources. In the middle, gates and control nodes redirect the streams. On the right, the streams merge into a concentrated reservoir that gains institutional scale. No labels baked into the image unless added later by the designer. Clean symbolic realism, visual economics meets occult philosophy, readable at book-page size, no corporate infographic clichés, no evil faces, no generic stock icons.

# PR-L026-005 — PLUTOCRACIA SEM CARICATURA

**Função:** capítulo sobre plutocracia

## Intenção
Representar a pergunta estrutural: quando riqueza concentrada se converte em capacidade decisória.

## Prompt
Serious editorial illustration about the conversion of concentrated wealth into decision-making power. A city or institutional landscape seen from above, with many distributed economic flows converging into a small set of elevated decision nodes connected to legislation chambers, infrastructure switches, media channels and financial gates. Avoid conspiracy imagery, secret cabal clichés, ethnic or religious coding, shadowy hooded elites, puppeteer hands, or literal dollar signs. The composition should show structure rather than accuse unnamed people. Philosophical, sociological, magical-symbolic, precise, restrained.

---

# PR-L026-006 — FRAGMENTAÇÃO HORIZONTAL / CONCENTRAÇÃO VERTICAL

**Função:** capítulo ou seção sobre fragmentação.

## Prompt
Editorial conceptual illustration of horizontal fragmentation and vertical power concentration. At ground level, many communities or social groups are separated into competing islands and channels, their connective bridges broken or diverted. Above them, a vertical structure remains coherent and receives energy from multiple disconnected streams. The point is structural: fragmented solidarity below, preserved concentration above. Do not imply a hidden mastermind; no puppet-master trope, no secret society symbols, no ethnic coding. Neutral analytical visual language with magical-philosophical atmosphere.

---

# PR-L026-007 — SATURNO + PLUTÃO: PRIMEIRO PRENDE, DEPOIS EXTRAI

**Função:** comparação planetária

## Prompt
Diptych-style conceptual editorial illustration. First phase: a system of rings, walls, contracts, routines and survival dependencies narrows the available exits without depicting a literal prison. Second phase: once movement is constrained, productive energy is redirected through extraction channels toward a concentrated reservoir. Saturnian visual language should emphasize boundary, repetition and inability to exit; Plutonian visual language should emphasize depth, capture, extraction and accumulation. Keep the two mechanisms visually distinct yet causally connected. No horror fantasy, no generic astrology poster.

# PR-L026-008 — TRÍADE SATURNO / PLUTÃO / NETUNO

**Função:** material editorial da série

## Prompt
Three-panel conceptual system for a modern syncretic magical-philosophical series. Saturn: constrained exits, loops and hard boundaries. Pluto: captured productive flows converging into concentrated reservoirs. Neptune: isolated perception fields, fogged connections and separated consciousness spaces suggesting alienation and impaired autonomous choice. Each panel must have its own visual grammar while belonging to one editorial family. No planet represented as inherently evil. No random occult decoration. Leave room for later labels: SAÍDA / POTÊNCIA / AUTODETERMINAÇÃO.

# PR-L026-009 — ECONOMIA PSÍQUICA

**Função:** ilustração interna

## Prompt
Conceptual map of psychic economy: attention, fear, desire, guilt, anger, faith, sexuality, prestige, knowledge, identity, belonging, time and creative force represented as distinct subtle streams emerging from many human lives and institutions. Some streams circulate reciprocally; others are captured and concentrated through control gates. The image must not claim these are measurable physical energies. It should look like a philosophical map of flows, not a scientific nervous system diagram. Elegant, readable, symbolic, modern occult editorial illustration.

# PR-L026-010 — AUTONOMIA COMO CONTRAPARTE

**Função:** seção ética

## Prompt
Editorial illustration of power without capture. Multiple autonomous sources hold their own reservoirs while exchanging resources through voluntary, visible, reversible connections. No central node owns the system. Depth and Plutonian transformation remain present: underground resources, pressure, regeneration, reserves and transmutation, but the visual structure shows agency and reciprocity rather than extraction. This is not a definitive image of “virtuous Pluto”; it is an exploratory visual question about how concentrated power might exist without violating autonomy.

**Status:** explicitamente exploratório porque a forma virtuosa ainda não está definida.
