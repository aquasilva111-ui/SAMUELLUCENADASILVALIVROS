**Status:** direção visual em exploração. Nenhuma imagem deste arquivo é capa final sem aprovação autoral.

## 1. Hierarquia de capa confirmada

A capa deve comunicar:

**THE POST WOMAN MEN**  
grande chamada/quote de marketing.

**THE MALE FROM THE ORANGE SPHERE**  
título real.

**Esferas do Poder**  
identificação da série.

A frase de marketing deve fisgar; o título deve explicar.

## 2. Princípio visual disponível

Para imagens conceituais, priorizar:
- sensação;
- ideia;
- símbolo;
- clareza conceitual;
- poucos elementos com forte função.

Não confundir isso com minimalismo obrigatório. O objetivo é evitar detalhe sem função.

### Prompt V1 — capa conceitual

Book cover concept for “THE MALE FROM THE ORANGE SPHERE”, part of the “Esferas do Poder” series. The dominant marketing line at the top reads “THE POST WOMAN MEN”, bold and immediately provocative. Below it, the actual book title “THE MALE FROM THE ORANGE SPHERE” appears with quieter intellectual authority. A single luminous orange sphere acts as the central symbolic object. The cover should communicate a masculinity organized by intuition rather than survival: calm, internally integrated, delicate without appearing weak, masculine without aggressive performance. Concept over decoration, strong negative space, editorial sophistication, contemporary philosophical psychology book, visually memorable at thumbnail size, no cliché alpha-male imagery, no sexualized woman, no chakra iconography unless explicitly requested later.

hypermasculine bodybuilder, manosphere aesthetic, red-pill iconography, misogynistic imagery, submissive women, luxury-car masculinity, tactical weapons, tribal alpha symbolism, sacral chakra symbol, rainbow pride symbolism used as shorthand for sexuality, literal brain illustration, cluttered collage, generic self-help stock photography.

### Prompt V1 — diagrama editorial

Create a clean conceptual editorial diagram for the theory “The Male from the Orange Sphere”. On one side, a neutral structural field labeled “Masculinidade de sobrevivência / Esfera Existencial”; on the other, a luminous orange sphere labeled “Masculinidade intuitiva / Esfera Laranja”. Between them, show a conceptual relation not as a hierarchy but as two organizational logics. Around the orange sphere, use small restrained labels: “intuição integrada”, “delicadeza”, “conexão emocional e intelectual”, “autonomia identitária diante da mulher”, “baixa identificação com masculino carnal”. Avoid chakra diagrams, evolutionary ladders, superiority arrows or scientific-looking claims.

## 6. Prompt editorial — retrato do homem da Esfera Laranja

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P003  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

Conceptual editorial portrait of a young adult man representing “The Male from the Orange Sphere”. He is clearly masculine but not posed through dominance, aggression or muscular spectacle. His presence is quiet, perceptive, self-contained and intuitive. The image should suggest delicacy without feminizing him, emotional depth without sadness, sensuality without sexual display, and autonomy without isolation. A subtle orange spherical light exists near or around him as an abstract field, not a chakra. The composition should feel like an intellectual magazine illustration: sensation before detail, clean symbolic structure, sophisticated negative space, no literal stereotypes of bisexuality, no female figure used as proof of his desire.

## 7. Prompt editorial — relação com mulheres

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P004  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

Conceptual illustration of a man and a woman in deep emotional and intellectual connection before physical desire. They are not embracing sexually; the visual focus is attention, conversation, mutual perception and harmony. The man is not incomplete without her: both figures retain clear independent spatial presence. An orange sphere or orange field can subtly connect the space between them, symbolizing intuitive relationality. The scene should communicate “choice and connection, not identity dependence”. Avoid romance-novel clichés, possessive body language, wedding symbolism or male-dominance composition.

## 8. Prompt editorial — homem sem tribo masculina

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P005  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

Conceptual editorial scene: one man stands near a group of men but does not visually belong to their social code. The group should not be demonized; simply show a difference in rhythm, posture and attention. The central man is calm, observant and internally coherent rather than rejected or humiliated. A restrained orange light marks his intuitive field. The image explores low identification with conventional male bonding, not superiority. Avoid bullying cliché, locker-room parody, caricatured macho men, victim imagery or loneliness melodrama.

## 9. Prompt editorial — “Post Woman Men”

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P006  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

Create a provocative conceptual cover/editorial image for the phrase “THE POST WOMAN MEN”. The central idea is not a world after women and not rejection of women. It is a man whose masculine identity remains structurally intact without requiring a woman as proof of being a man. Use an autonomous male figure and an independent female figure occupying separate but harmonious fields, with no visual dependency chain. The orange sphere belongs to the man’s internal organization, not to possession of the woman. The image must feel intellectually provocative rather than hostile.

## 10. Prompt abstrato — esfera laranja

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P007  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

An abstract orange sphere suspended in a large quiet field, radiating layered translucent structures that suggest intuition, relational perception and internal coherence. No chakra symbol, no occult sigils, no literal neurons, no cosmic cliché. The sphere should feel psychologically meaningful but not pseudo-scientific. Editorial conceptual art, strong silhouette, restrained texture, idea-first composition.

## 11. Prompt para ensaio fotográfico/editorial

**Prompt ID:** L008-TMOS-P008  
**Status:** PROVISÓRIO

High-fashion conceptual editorial portrait series about a masculinity beyond survival performance. A male subject appears relaxed, attentive, elegant and physically present without dominance poses. Use gesture, eye direction and negative space to communicate internal security. Orange appears as controlled light, material or geometry rather than decorative saturation. The editorial should explore intuition, autonomy and relational openness. No macho luxury codes, no aggressive styling, no victim softness, no romantic dependency.
